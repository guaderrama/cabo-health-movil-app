# CaboHealth 3.0/build 4 submission gates

This metadata package is internally consistent but intentionally **not submission-ready**. Do not upload/select/build from it as if the gates below were complete.

## Hard external gate: seller/legal healthcare provider

App Store Connect currently shows an **Individual** membership and individual legal entity, while the product is CaboHealth and the primary category is Medical. Submission is blocked until one of these is documented:

1. membership is converted to the actual registered organization providing CaboHealth services and the seller matches that legal provider;
2. the app is transferred to that correct organization; or
3. Apple gives written direction accepting the exact proposed relationship.

A brand authorization letter alone may answer the copycat/brand question but does not by itself resolve the healthcare-provider seller requirement. Do not relabel the app or category merely to conceal the relationship.
