## Description
CaboHealth brings two carefully curated public wellness resources into one calm, bilingual experience.

HEALTHY RECIPES
Browse twelve original recipe ideas with search and category filters. Each recipe includes ingredients, steps, timing, servings, allergen notes, and food-safety reminders. Recipe favorites stay on your device.

CABOHEALTH CLINIC GUIDE
Prepare for a clinic visit with public location, hours, contact options, language and accessibility guidance, an emergency limitation, and a practical checklist. The checklist stays on your device and has no free-text fields or uploads.

MORE VALUE IN THE APP
• A built-in recipe browser and visit-preparation checklist remain useful even when the hosted resources are unavailable.
• Save either public resource for quick return.
• Choose English or Spanish (Mexico), light, dark, or system appearance, and reduced visual effects.
• Restricted navigation opens only the two reviewed CaboHealth resources.

The content is general educational information, not an emergency service or a substitute for guidance from a qualified professional.
