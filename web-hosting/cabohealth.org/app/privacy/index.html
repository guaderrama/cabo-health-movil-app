Privacy notice

What this covers

This notice covers the two public modules, Healthy Recipes and CaboHealth Clinic Guide, when delivered inside the CaboHealth app through WebKit or opened on the public website.

Operator

Cabo Health is a trade brand owned and operated by Ivan Guaderrama. It is not a separate legal entity. Ivan Guaderrama is the operator responsible for this notice and can be contacted through the address below.

Information boundary

The two public miniapps do not request or collect health information. They use no account, no app-generated identifiers, no analytics, no advertising, and no sensitive permissions. There is no field or form for personal or clinical details.

On-device choices

Favorites and preferences remain on your device. They support local interface choices and are not sent to CaboHealth by either public module.

Hosting logs

Prior technical verification observed Hostinger access-log fields for IP address, timestamp, HTTP method, requested resource, device and user-agent details, country, response bytes, and latency. The owner separately confirmed that Hostinger logs IP address, browser or user-agent, requested URL, and technical errors. These records are used to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the public pages. They are visible in hPanel for up to 7 days. Any additional retention and deletion are controlled by Hostinger.

Children and age

The two public modules are general educational resources. They offer no account and no way to submit a child's personal or health information.

Questions and content reports

For a privacy question or to Report Content, email ivan@ivanguaderrama.com. Do not include health or other sensitive information. The content report process is described on Report Content.

Policy changes

Material changes will be published at this canonical page before a corresponding public release. Earlier wording will not be presented as describing a changed build.

Responsable

Cabo Health es una marca comercial propiedad de Ivan Guaderrama y operada por él. No es una entidad legal separada. Ivan Guaderrama es el responsable de este aviso y puede ser contactado mediante la dirección indicada abajo.

Límite de información

Las dos miniapps públicas no solicitan ni recopilan información de salud. Funcionan sin cuenta, sin identificadores generados por la app, sin analítica, sin publicidad y sin permisos sensibles. No hay campos ni formularios para datos personales o clínicos.

Los dos módulos públicos son recursos educativos generales. No ofrecen una cuenta ni una manera de enviar información personal o de salud de un menor.
